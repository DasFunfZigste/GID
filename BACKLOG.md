# Backlog

The first version focuses on personal tasks, clear dashboard reminders, private accounts, explicit sharing, and retained completion history.

Potential later additions, in order of usefulness:

- Recurring tasks with a clear history for each occurrence.
- Optional due-date notifications, with delivery and timing chosen by the administrator.
- Small checklists within a task.
- A guided backup download and restore workflow.

Keep future changes within the small, single-container deployment unless requirements justify more infrastructure.
